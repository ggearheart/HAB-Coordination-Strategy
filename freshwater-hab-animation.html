How freshwater harmful algal blooms develop

The same drivers act in streams, rivers, and reservoirs — but the outcome depends on residence time, the length of time water lingers. As flow slows and water sits longer, nutrients and warmth tip the system into a bloom. Each stage is tagged with the Water Board program whose authority bears on it.

Reading the animation. Cells are always present; a bloom is a matter of degree. In fast, well-flushed streams the same nutrients tend to express as benthic mats on the bed rather than open-water scums; in long-residence reservoirs they accumulate as planktonic surface scums. Residence time is the variable the Boards most directly influence, through flow. This is a simplified schematic, not to scale.
